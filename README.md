# Customer Churn & Retention Analysis

## Business Problem
A company wanted to identify customers likely to discontinue services and understand the major factors contributing to customer churn.

---

## Objective
- Analyse churn patterns
- Identify high-risk customer segments
- Evaluate customer retention trends
- Generate recommendations to improve retention

---

## Dataset Information
- Dataset Size: ~7,000 customer records
- Tools Used: SQL, Excel, Tableau

---

## Key Analysis Performed
- Churn rate calculation
- Customer segmentation analysis
- Revenue impact analysis
- Retention trend analysis

---

## Key Insights
- Customers with shorter tenure showed higher churn probability
- Certain service categories had significantly higher churn rates
- Contract type strongly influenced customer retention

---

## Recommendations
- Improve engagement for new customers
- Offer retention incentives for high-risk segments
- Optimize service plans for better customer satisfaction
